About us

What is the club

The Alumni Club is the independent membership body for everyone who studied here — forty-two city chapters, twelve thousand verified members, and a directory that has been maintained continuously since 1958. It is run by members, funded by dues, and answers to nobody else.

Our membership programme

Membership is designed to be useful rather than ceremonial. It gets you into the directory, into a city chapter that meets whether or not you show up, and into a mentorship pool that pairs roughly 310 people a year. Verification takes a day. What you do with it after that is up to you.

Why us

Three standing programmes your dues actually pay for.

01
City chapters

Forty-two of them, each run by members who live there. A dinner every month, a bigger night every quarter, and someone to call when you land in a new city.

02
Mentorship pairs

Around 310 matches a year across industries. Six sessions, a clear ask on the table, and both sides opting in — not a networking roulette wheel.

03
The reunion

One weekend a year, back on campus, every batch at once. Milestone years get their own dinner. Everyone else gets the lawn and the long bar.
